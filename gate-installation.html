Gates

Gate Installation and Repair in Albuquerque, NM

A gate is the part of the fence you touch every day, and it is the part the wind works on. We hang walk gates and drive gates on posts and hinges sized for the leaf. A sagging gate is usually a hardware or post problem, not a latch problem.

When you need a new gate or a rebuilt one

New fences get their gates in the same job. Call about a gate alone when the fence is fine and the opening is not: it drags, it will not latch, the hinge post has leaned, or the opening was never wide enough for the truck or the mower.

Albuquerque wind is hard on solid gates. A wide privacy gate on a light post will sag. We size the post before we hang a bigger leaf. Automatic openers are a separate piece of equipment. We build a leaf that can carry one. We do not pretend an opener fixes a weak frame.

How a gate is hung

  1. Measure the opening

    Clear width, swing direction, slope, and what has to pass through.

  2. Check the posts

    If the hinge post is moving, a new hinge on that post will not last.

  3. Build or fit the leaf

    The gate matches the fence: wood, vinyl, chain link, or metal.

  4. Set hardware and test

    Hinges, latch, and a drop rod on double gates. Then we open it like you will.

What changes the cost

We do not publish a price list. These are the details that move an estimate.

Walk or drive

A three-foot walk gate and a twelve-foot drive gate are different structures.

The material

A wood privacy gate, a chain-link gate, and a steel gate do not share a price.

New posts

If the hinge post has to be replaced, that digging is part of the quote.

Hardware

Heavy hinges, adjustable hardware, and drop rods cost more than a light residential hinge.

Questions before you call

How much does a fence gate cost in Albuquerque?

A single walk gate on a sound fence is the small job. A new drive gate with new posts is closer to a small fence project. Material, width, and whether the posts get replaced decide the quote. We look at the opening before we price it.

Can you fix a gate that sags or drags?

Yes, when the frame is still square enough to save. We reset or replace the hinge post, upgrade the hinges, and rehang the leaf. If the gate itself is twisted or rotten, a new leaf is the repair. Tightening the latch again is not.

Do you install walk gates and drive gates?

Yes. Walk gates for people and mowers, drive gates for vehicles, and double swings where one leaf is not enough. The fence material does not have to change for us to replace the gate.

Do I need a bigger post because of the wind?

On a solid gate, usually yes. Open chain link and aluminum shed wind. A solid wood or vinyl leaf does not. Spring wind on the West Mesa and open lots will bend a light hinge post. The post is part of the gate quote for that reason.

Can you hang a gate on the fence I already have?

Yes, if the fence can carry it. We check the posts on either side of the opening. Sound posts can take a new leaf. Leaning or rotten posts get replaced first. We do not bolt a new gate to a post that is already failing.

Talk through the line

Call or send the lot details. We will tell you if the job is a repair, a replacement, or a different material than you started with.
