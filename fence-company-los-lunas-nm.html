Fence Company in Los Lunas, NM

Los Lunas has both newer subdivision yards and older properties with irrigation. Wind still reaches these lots. A fence quote here starts with which kind of parcel it is, because the Village of Los Lunas and the county are not the same jurisdiction as Albuquerque.

What fence jobs look like in Los Lunas

Newer streets usually want backyard privacy in wood or vinyl, plus a gate, sometimes as the first real fence after the builder left the yard open. HOA sheets show up in some of those neighborhoods and have to be read before the order.

Older and edge lots may have a longer line, an irrigation ditch, or a tired chain-link fence that needs repair rather than a full privacy replacement. We will say when repair is the better spend.

Materials that fit these lots

Wood and vinyl for privacy yards. Chain link where a practical enclosure is enough. Farm-style wire or rail only when the property is actually keeping animals. Aluminum for an open front or side line.

Permits and local rules

Work inside the Village of Los Lunas follows village rules. County parcels nearby do not. Do not assume an Albuquerque front-yard height. We identify the jurisdiction from the address and note it on the estimate so the permit step is the local one.

Questions from Los Lunas

How much does a fence cost in Los Lunas?

A standard new backyard is priced from footage, material, and gates. Irrigated or older lots can add removal, softer digging, or a longer run. Those are different quotes, and we keep them that way.

Can you repair an older fence instead of replacing it?

Yes, when the posts are sound. A lot of calls south of Albuquerque are tired wood or loose chain link, not a need for an all-new line. We check the posts and recommend the smaller job when it will last.

Do you treat Los Lunas like an Albuquerque city lot?

No. The permit path and a lot of the yards are different. The wind and the sun are similar, so the post and material advice carries over. The paperwork does not.
