Fence Company in Edgewood, NM

Edgewood sits higher and colder than the Albuquerque valley, and the wind has a clear run at a lot of these parcels. Fences here are often longer, and a freeze that the valley shrugs off still matters for posts and gates.

What fence jobs look like in Edgewood

Ranch and large-lot residential lines need rail, wire, or a practical enclosure more often than a short privacy fence. House yards still want a screen, but that section should stay near the house instead of trying to privacy-fence an acre.

Freeze-thaw and wind both work on gates and on posts that were set shallow. We treat post depth and gate hardware as part of the design, not as a valley spec driven up the hill unchanged.

Materials that fit these lots

Farm fence for animals and long boundaries. Wood where a house yard needs privacy and someone will maintain the finish in this sun and wind. Metal and aluminum for open frontages. Chain link for a straight utility yard.

Permits and local rules

The Town of Edgewood is in Santa Fe County and uses its own rules. Do not import an Albuquerque zoning answer. Large parcels can also cross easements and driveways that change where a gate belongs. We lay the gate out for the driveway you use in winter, not only for a summer photo.

Questions from Edgewood

How much does a fence cost in Edgewood?

Longer lines and wind exposure are the cost drivers. A house-yard screen is a smaller number than fencing the parcel. Rock, slope, and a heavy gate add to either one. We quote after the layout, not from a valley per-foot habit.

Can you build a fence that stays put in the wind?

That is the requirement, not an upgrade. Open fences shed wind better. Solid sections get posts and rails that match the height. We will recommend open material on the exposed side when a solid wall is going to fight the weather every spring.

Do you fence horse property in Edgewood?

Yes. Rail, pipe, and horse-safe wire, with gates that a truck can use. A decorative metal front fence and a horse fence can both be on the same property. They are not the same bid.
