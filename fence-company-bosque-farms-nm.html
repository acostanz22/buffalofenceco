Fence Company in Bosque Farms, NM

Bosque Farms properties are village lots with room, often with animals and irrigation near the river. Fence lines are longer than a city backyard, and the ground near the bosque is not West Mesa caliche.

What fence jobs look like in Bosque Farms

The useful fence is often a combination: wire or rail for animals, and a separate residential section at the house. Drive gates matter because the vehicles are trucks and trailers, not only cars.

Soft, irrigated soil needs posts set for that ground. A hole that would be right in hard caliche can be wrong in river-bottom dirt. We also keep fence lines from cutting off ditch access.

Materials that fit these lots

Farm fence first where animals are the reason for the call. Wood or chain link at the house. Vinyl when someone wants a low-maintenance privacy section and the run is not a pasture. Gates built wide enough to use.

Permits and local rules

The Village of Bosque Farms is its own jurisdiction in Valencia County. Albuquerque wall permits do not transfer. Longer agricultural lines can also involve ditch easements that a village fence permit will not spell out for you. We ask about both.

Questions from Bosque Farms

How much does fencing cost in Bosque Farms?

Length is the first driver, then whether the line is wire, rail, or a residential privacy fence. A house yard alone is a smaller quote than fencing the acreage. Gates for trucks are called out on their own.

Can you fence animals on irrigated ground?

Yes. The post plan follows softer soil, and the wire or rail follows the animal. We do not use a backyard privacy spec for a pasture just because the house is on the same parcel.

Do you work around existing ditches?

Yes. Show us the ditch before layout. The fence stays off the access the ditch needs, even if that means the line is not a perfect rectangle on a satellite photo.
