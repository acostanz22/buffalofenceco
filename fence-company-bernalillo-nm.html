Fence Company in Bernalillo, NM

Bernalillo fence work splits in two. In-town yards need a normal residential line. Parcels toward the river or up on the open ground need longer runs, more wind thought, and sometimes an animal fence.

What fence jobs look like in Bernalillo

Town lots are backyard privacy, chain link, and a gate, with the Town of Bernalillo’s rules rather than Albuquerque’s. The quote is still about footage and soil, not about copying a city permit answer onto a different town.

Outside the tighter streets, lines get longer and more exposed. Wind off the open ground punishes a tall solid fence on light posts. If the property keeps animals, we switch to rail or wire instead of stretching privacy panels down a long boundary.

Materials that fit these lots

Wood and vinyl for in-town privacy. Chain link for practical yards. Farm fence where the parcel is doing agricultural work. Gates sized for the vehicles that use the property, which on the larger lots often means a drive gate, not only a walk gate.

Permits and local rules

The Town of Bernalillo and Sandoval County are not the City of Albuquerque. Ask the town which height and which permit fit that street before ordering a tall front-yard fence. We build to the answer for that address.

Questions from Bernalillo

How much does fencing cost in Bernalillo?

In-town backyard replacements are priced like other house lots. Longer exposed lines and farm sections cost according to footage and material. We look at which of the two you have before we quote.

Can you fence a lot that is part yard and part acreage?

Yes. The house yard and the outer line can be different fences. That is often cheaper and more useful than one tall wall around everything.

Do you use Albuquerque permit rules here?

No. Bernalillo has its own process. We do not copy the city’s three-foot front-yard figure onto a town parcel. The estimate notes the jurisdiction so the permit step matches the address.
