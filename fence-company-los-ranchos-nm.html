Fence Company in Los Ranchos, NM

Los Ranchos is a village, not a city neighborhood. Lots are larger, horses are common, and irrigation ditches cut through properties that also have a house yard. The fence has to respect both uses.

What fence jobs look like in Los Ranchos

The house might need a wood or chain-link yard fence while the rest of the acreage needs rail or wire. Treating the whole parcel like a subdivision backyard either underbuilds the animal side or overbuilds the house side.

Irrigation is the other constraint. Soft ground near ditches does not hold a post the way caliche does, and the ditch itself needs access. We set posts for the soil that is actually there and keep the line off the maintenance path.

Materials that fit these lots

Rail, wire, and pipe for the animals. Wood, chain link, or a short picket for the house. Aluminum works where you want an open line near the home without a solid wall. Vinyl is available, but it is not the default on these lots.

Permits and local rules

The Village of Los Ranchos de Albuquerque has its own ordinances. Do not use a City of Albuquerque front-yard rule on a village parcel without checking. We ask which jurisdiction the address is actually in, because the North Valley line between the city and the village is easy to mix up.

Questions from Los Ranchos

How much does a fence cost in Los Ranchos?

A house yard and a pasture are often both on the quote. Footage on the acreage, the animal mesh or rail, and a residential section around the home are priced separately. Ditch crossings and soft ground add labor.

Can you keep horses in without a solid wall?

Yes. Rail or a horse-safe wire is the right tool. A solid privacy fence around a whole Los Ranchos acreage is expensive and wrong for the animals. We keep privacy, if you want it, next to the house.

Do you work on ditch-side property lines?

Yes, with the ditch called out first. The fence can follow the property and still leave room for ditch work. We will not string a line across an acequia and figure it out later.
