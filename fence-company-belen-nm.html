Fence Company in Belen, NM

Belen work is a mix of older in-town yards and acreage around the city. A lot of the calls are repairs: wood that has dried out, chain link that has sagged, and gates that drag. New lines are quoted when the posts are done.

What fence jobs look like in Belen

In-town yards often have an existing fence that has been patched already. We sort repair from replacement before selling a full new run. Wind is still a factor on open edges of town, so a rebuilt privacy fence gets real posts.

Properties outside the tighter blocks may need a longer practical fence, chain link or farm wire, rather than a decorative panel. The City of Belen and the county parcels around it are planned separately.

Materials that fit these lots

Repair across wood, chain link, and metal. New wood or vinyl where a yard needs privacy. Chain link for a straight enclosure. Farm fencing when the land is being used for animals. Gates repaired or rebuilt with the fence.

Permits and local rules

The City of Belen handles city parcels. Valencia County handles land outside it. Neither one inherits Albuquerque’s ordinance. We confirm which one applies before treating a front-yard height as settled.

Questions from Belen

How much does fence repair cost in Belen?

A gate and a few sections are priced as a repair. A full side of failed posts is priced closer to a new fence. We say which one it is after we see the line, so you are not paying twice.

Can you install a new fence on an older lot?

Yes. Removal of the old material is part of that quote. Older lots sometimes have odd corners, trees, and grade along an alley. Those details change the footage and the post count.

Do you fence acreage outside Belen?

Yes, when the drive still makes sense for the job. Longer animal and chain-link lines are the usual request. If the property is too far for a small repair, we will tell you when you call.
