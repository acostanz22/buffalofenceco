Privacy Policy

This page explains what the estimate form collects and how it is used.

The quote form is embedded from Tally. It asks for the details you type, such as your name and how to reach you, plus notes about the fence job. A hidden field named source is set to Albuquerque Fence Company so estimate requests from this website can be told apart from requests sent on other sites.

We use that information to reply about the fence estimate. Do not send payment card numbers or other financial account details through the form. Phone calls go to (505) 210-9903 and are not stored by this website.

Tally processes the form submission under its own terms. This site does not run a customer account system, and it does not sell mailing lists. If you want a submitted request deleted, call (505) 210-9903 and say which request.
